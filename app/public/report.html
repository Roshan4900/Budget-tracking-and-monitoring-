Budget vs progress report

Is the money being spent in line with the work actually delivered? A project-wise and district-wise efficiency check · FY 2081/82

How to read this report
Each item compares budget used (how much money has been spent) against work done (physical progress on the ground). When spending races ahead of progress, the budget can run out before the work is finished. For example, a project with Rs 40 Cr that has spent Rs 38 Cr (95%) but completed only 30% of the work has just 5% of its budget left for 70% of the remaining work — a clear overspend.
Efficient — work ahead of spending
On track — spending matches progress
Watch — spending ahead of progress
Over budget — spending far ahead of progress
